Security — নিরাপত্তা

Lets staff check what an item cost while agreeing a price at the counter. It appears as a small unlabelled dot on the cart line and only shows the figure when clicked, so it isn't readable over a customer's shoulder. This switch alone shows it to nobody — each role must also be given POS Purchase Price → View under Settings → Roles. Roles without it never receive the cost from the server at all.
